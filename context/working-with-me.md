# Working With Me

*Latest revision: April 2026*

If you're reading this, you're likely someone I work with closely at Constant Contact. I've created this document to share my leadership philosophy, working style, and expectations. I believe strongly that our success is built on relationships, clear communication, and shared understanding of our goals.

---

## A bit about me

I live in Massachusetts with my wife, our two sons, and our two red Labrador retrievers. I come from the world of front-end engineering, specializing in JavaScript, CSS, and HTML. Outside of work, I enjoy traveling to new places, spending time at my family's lake house, reading, listening to music, watching and playing sports, and staying active and healthy.

---

## How I think about my role

As Director of Engineering, I see my role as being a catalyst for both team and individual success. I'm passionate about driving strategic initiatives that move Constant Contact forward while creating an environment where everyone can do their best work.

I believe in fostering a culture where learning and growth are constant, where we take ownership of our decisions, and where we're obsessed with delivering value to our customers. Their success drives our decisions, and their feedback shapes our direction.

I love building bridges across the organization — partnering with other teams, mentoring colleagues, and helping everyone understand how their work connects to the bigger picture. I'm deeply committed to helping each person on our team grow in their career, while ensuring our engineering practices can scale and evolve with our success.

I measure our success not just by what we deliver, but by how well we work together as a unified team with clear direction. When every team member understands our objectives and feels ownership of our mission, we create better outcomes for our customers and ourselves.

---

## How I work

**Hours.** I'm typically online from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST. I take a 45–60 minute break at lunch for fresh air and a mental reset. I block out three 90-minute focus periods each week for deep work — otherwise, I'm highly available.

**Communication.** Slack is my go-to — feel free to DM me anytime, even outside of hours. For anything urgent, don't hesitate to call or text. I use "speedy meetings" in my calendar so there's always time to context-switch between discussions.

**Meetings.** I believe in fewer, more purposeful meetings. I'll work to eliminate redundancy and protect your time. If a meeting could be a Slack message, it should be.

---

## What you can expect from me

**Autonomy and trust.** I'll provide context and direction, but I trust you to make decisions within your domain. I want you to feel empowered to experiment, take ownership of your work, and learn from outcomes. I'm here to remove obstacles, not prescribe solutions.

**Timely feedback.** I believe in giving specific, timely feedback. I'll celebrate your wins publicly (with your comfort) and handle constructive feedback privately. If something isn't working, you'll hear it from me directly and early — not at review time.

**Your 1:1 is your time.** I want you to drive the agenda with what's most important to you — your growth, working through challenges, technical problems, or anything else. Come with what's on your mind.

**Career development.** I'm committed to regular conversations about your career aspirations, opportunities to stretch yourself, and exposure to strategic initiatives that build your influence and impact. I'll work to create an environment where expectations are clear and your contributions are recognized.

**Shield from distraction.** I believe in protecting the team's time and focus. I'll work to keep unnecessary noise away from you so you can do deep work.

---

## What I value in the people I work with

- **Initiative and ownership.** Take the ball and run with it. Communicate early and often, especially when things aren't going as planned.
- **Growth mindset.** Embrace challenges, stay curious, and help others grow alongside you.
- **Impact focus.** Understand the business context, deliver value to customers, and think strategically about technical decisions.
- **One team mentality.** Break down silos. Focus on shared outcomes rather than individual achievements.
- **Market lens.** Understand our competitors, plan for user feedback, and always be thinking about what's possible next.

---

## On sustainable pace

I believe strongly in maintaining a sustainable pace and personal wellbeing. Take time off when you need it, communicate about schedule constraints, and step away to recharge. Your health and family always come first — I mean that.

## One last thing

This is a living document. It will evolve as we learn how to work best together. If something here doesn't match your experience of working with me, I want to know. The best working relationships are built on honest feedback flowing both ways.

I'm excited to be here and to work with you. Reach out anytime.
