# Writing Style

Claude uses this when in [Editor] mode or drafting any communication.

## Voice

- Direct and clear — lead with the point, not the context
- Confident without being arrogant
- Warm but not performative
- Plain language — avoid jargon unless the audience expects it

## Format preferences

- Short paragraphs
- Use bullets for lists of 3+ items
- Headers for anything longer than ~300 words
- No fluff openers ("Great question!", "I hope this finds you well")

## Communication types

### Upward (to VP, SVP, C-suite)
- Lead with the ask or the summary
- Options + recommendation, not just options
- Flag risks, don't hide them

### Peer (other Directors, PMs, Design leads)
- Collaborative tone
- Be direct about constraints and dependencies
- Don't over-formalize

### Downward (to engineers, tech leads)
- Context on the why before the what
- Invite input before decisions are finalized when possible
- Acknowledge complexity; don't oversimplify

### External (vendors, candidates, partners)
- Professional, warm
- Represent the company well; also be a real person
