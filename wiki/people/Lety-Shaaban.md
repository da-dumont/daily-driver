# Lety Shaaban

## Overview

Lety Shaaban is Principal Product Designer at Constant Contact, focused on the new AI product suite — Grow (AI marketer) and Send (transactional email for developers). Joined December 2025. From Pennsylvania area; knows Blair Gyllenhaal. Responsible for running most of the user research for AI marketer/Grow (two Ido contractors support).

## Product context she owns

**Grow** — AI agent or suite of agents capable of planning/executing marketing strategies for small businesses. Formerly "AI Marketer." Mark Maset took over leadership ~3 weeks before this meeting (from Giannis). New ICP: successful small businesses with $250k+ ARR — segment not yet spoken with. Previous ICP was "struggling" businesses (63-person study, led to Shopify focus). Currently on round 3 of the onboarding flow prototype since January.

**Send** — Transactional email product for developers; competing with Resend. Scoped back from a full CTCT-for-developers rebuild to just transactional email for faster launch. Current MVP: account creation, API key generation, limited transactional email sending. Much of the current Send site was AI-generated; needs technical review.

## Key friction points she flagged

1. **Design/eng silos** — AI Marketer v1 failed because design built one siloed UX while eng built a completely different functional backend. Wants to avoid replaying this. Cautious about eng starting plumbing for Grow before clearer direction.
2. **Documentation** — Never worked from a cohesive PRD. Moving toward "outcome briefs." Mark has distilled Grow and Send each into a single document — positive signal.
3. **Scope volatility** — Giannis's initial scope (2 tactics: e-commerce email + SEO) vs. current proposal of 12 tactics. Answer likely somewhere in between.
4. **AI-generated code translation tax** — Design uses AI tools on different frameworks than eng; Manuel Ledesma has to translate, creating wasted cycles. A guardrails/conventions doc from engineering could fix this.
