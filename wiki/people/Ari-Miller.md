# Ari Miller

## Overview

Ari Miller is the Architect for the Grow (AI marketer) product at Constant Contact. Based in New Jersey. Has been at CTCT ~2.5 years; joined to address legacy system debt. Currently focused on the "harness" for the AI marketer v2 agent system. Working primarily with Marc Maset on the growth initiative. Not yet using Jira for the current fast-paced project.

## Philosophy

Strongly aligned with rapid prototyping — can get a prototype deployed in 15 minutes to gather feedback. Emphasizes that "shipping often and fast" requires proper upfront design and evaluation setup, especially for AI. Believes the bottleneck is getting the right harness/evaluation framework, not the features themselves.

Key framing: AI marketer v1 was "too agent-driven" — too complicated, hard to reason about, hard to measure. v2 is focused on building the right *harness*: a foundation that measures whether capabilities work, leveraging integrations and tools (e.g., CTCT for email sending). Success = the harness, not the features.

Sees ideal working state as: agents do the work, humans review. Wants time for "meta work" — RFCs, system of record setup (referenced Strike Minions / Ramp as models).

## Patterns

- Prefers async documentation and system-of-record thinking alongside speed
- Opinionated about evaluation setup before shipping AI features
- Good signal on "what's been tried before" — useful for understanding v1 failures
